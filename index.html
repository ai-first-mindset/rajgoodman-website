About

Meet Raj

Raj Goodman Anand is the founder of AI-First Mindset® and Goodman Lantern. With an engineering background and a PhD thesis on AI written two decades ago, Raj has worked with AI since long before it went mainstream, helping leaders bring it into the way their businesses already run.

  • EO leadershipChapter President, EO Bangkok Metropolitan (2024–25). Regional Expert for Marketing and Communications, EO APAC (2026–27).
  • EO and YPO workshopsWorkshops and keynotes for EO and YPO chapters and forums.
  • Founder and entrepreneurScaled multiple startups, including GoPinLeads (25,000+ users).
  • Author and award winnerSeveral books on AI. BusinessWeek Europe's Young Entrepreneur of the Year.
