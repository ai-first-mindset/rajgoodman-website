[ 01 ]Why It Stalls

Transformation is a people problem first

New systems fail not because the technology is wrong, but because the organization isn’t ready. Hidden barriers — managers lacking data literacy, fear of job losses, confusion around tools — quietly slow every rollout. Raj surfaces these early, before they derail the change.

With empathy and structure, Raj helps leaders build agile, human-centered cultures where teams are keen to test new ideas — so transformation lands and lasts.
