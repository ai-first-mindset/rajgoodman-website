[ 10 ]FAQs

Questions, answered

What is AI strategy consulting?
AI strategy consulting helps businesses use AI to gain competitive advantages, optimise operations, and drive innovation. Raj Goodman offers expert guidance on AI implementation, data strategy, and organisational transformation.
How can AI benefit my business?
AI can boost efficiency, enhance decision-making, improve customer experiences, and even unlock new revenue streams. As an AI business consultant, Raj will help you identify high-impact AI applications tailored to your industry and business goals.
Do we need in-house AI expertise to work with Raj Goodman?
No. Raj will bridge the gap between your business needs and AI capabilities, translating complex AI concepts into actionable strategies regardless of your current technical expertise.
How long does it take to see results from AI implementation?
Some AI implementations will show immediate impact, while others may take months to integrate fully. Timelines vary based on project scope and complexity.
Is my data ready for AI?
Data readiness is crucial for AI success. Raj's AI consulting services will assess your data infrastructure, identify gaps, and develop a roadmap to ensure your data supports your AI ambitions, maximising the value from existing data while planning for your future needs.
