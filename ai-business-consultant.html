Almost any AI pilot looks promising in a controlled environment. The hard part is the shop floor — the messy reality where most proofs of concept quietly fail. Raj has spent decades integrating AI into legacy systems and operations, and he knows what makes it survive first contact.

As an AI business consultant, Raj works alongside your teams to move beyond experiments to scalable systems that improve accuracy, execution and ROI — with the people side handled, not assumed.
